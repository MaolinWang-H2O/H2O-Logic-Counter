## 0. Why This Calculator / 为什么需要这个计算器

*After a month at a logistics hub, I noticed something: everyone was busy, but the time actually spent on real output was less than one-third. The rest was consumed by waiting, searching, rework, and repeated communication. This isn't a people problem — it's a process problem.*

在快递站蹲了一个月之后，我发现一个现象：兄弟们每天都在忙，从早忙到晚，但真正在“干活”的时间，可能不到三分之一。剩下的时间，消耗在等货、找货、重复沟通、来回返工上。这不是人的问题，是流程的问题。

*I distilled this into a formula:*

我把这个观察抽象成了一个公式：

> **Logic Concentration = Theoretical Minimum Time ÷ Actual Time × 100%**
>
> **逻辑浓度 = 理论最短时间 ÷ 实际耗时 × 100%**

*It answers one question: **How much of your time is actually converting into output?** *

它回答一个问题：**你投入的时间里，有多少比例真正转化成了产出？**

**Scenario / 场景：** *A report that would take 20 minutes in ideal conditions, but actually took 60 minutes due to interruptions, rework, and waiting for feedback.*

- Theoretical Minimum Time / 理论最短时间：20 min / 分钟
- Actual Time / 实际耗时：60 min / 分钟
- **Logic Concentration / 逻辑浓度：33%**
- **Diagnosis / 诊断：Sticky · Logic is idling / 黏糊级 · 逻辑在空转**

*You put in 60 minutes, but only 20 minutes produced real output. The other 40 minutes were consumed somewhere in the process — waiting for approvals, rework, back-and-forth coordination. **You already know where.** *

投入 60 分钟，只有 20 分钟在真正产出。另外 40 分钟消耗在流程中的某个环节上——可能是等批、可能是反复确认、可能是来回折腾。**你心里应该已经有答案了。**

*Find the step that makes you want to sigh first — that's your bottleneck. Until that bottleneck is removed, you're losing 40 minutes every day.*

找一下你流程中“最想叹气的第一步”——那就是卡点。卡点不除，你每天多花 40 分钟。
