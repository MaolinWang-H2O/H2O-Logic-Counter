## 0. Why This Tool / 为什么需要这个提纯器

After a month at a logistics hub, I noticed something: everyone was busy, but the time actually spent on real output was less than one-third. The rest was consumed by waiting, searching, rework, and repeated communication. This isn't a people problem — it's a process problem. And the same is true of language: **most copy looks substantial but carries little substance.**

在快递站蹲了一个月之后，我发现一个现象：兄弟们每天都在忙，从早忙到晚，但真正在"干活"的时间，可能不到三分之一。剩下的时间，消耗在等货、找货、重复沟通、来回返工上。这不是人的问题，是流程的问题。文字也一样——大多数文案看起来很满，其实没多少干货。

I distilled this into a tool. It does not tell you "write faster." It tells you **where the fluff is.**

我把它做成了这个工具：它不告诉你"写快点"，它告诉你**"水分在哪里"**。

*(In H₂O's framing, fluff is "water" — empty claims that add weight but no value. The tool evaporates it.)*

*（在 H₂O 的框架里，fluff 就是"水"——只增重量不增价值的空话。提纯器把它蒸发掉。）*

## 2. Six Dimensions / 六维度量

The engine scores your text on six dimensions, each 0–100:

引擎从六个维度给文本打分，每项 0–100：

| Dimension | Question it answers | 维度 | 回答的问题 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Clarity** | Is it unambiguous and actionable? | **清晰度** | 表述是否无歧义、可执行？ |
| **Consistency** | Is it internally self-consistent? | **一致性** | 内部是否自洽、有无矛盾？ |
| **Traceability** | Can every claim be traced to a source? | **可追溯** | 能否追踪到源头依据？ |
| **Actionability** | Does it give a concrete instruction? | **可执行** | 是否给出明确动作指令？ |
| **Empathy & Responsibility** | Does it show care for people? | **爱与责任** | 是否体现对人的关怀？ |
| **Authenticity & Gratitude** | Is it grounded in honesty? | **真诚与感恩** | 是否守住真诚底色？ |

The average of the six becomes the Logic Concentration score; weak dimensions (<50) trigger tailored advice.

六项平均分即"逻辑浓度"总分；低于 50 的短板维度会触发针对性建议。

| Score | Tier | Meaning | 分数 | 档位 | 含义 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|

| 61 – 75 | ☁️ **Vapor** | Self-consistent logic, executable | 61 – 75 | ☁️ **气态** | 逻辑自洁，可执行 |
| > 75 | ⭐ **Super** | Axiom-grade — worthy of your playbook | > 75 | ⭐ **超浓** | 公理级，可写入手册 |

The displayed score is capped by the fluff rate, so a text full of hype can never score high no matter how polished it looks.

显示分受"水分率"钳制——水分高的文本再顺眼也打不高。
